ABOUT CBS

Education With Purpose

Calvary Baptist Schools (CBS) is a school dedicated to providing quality education and developing students academically, morally and socially.

For over 20 years, the school has continued to provide an environment where students can learn, grow and develop the skills they need for the future.

Located in Kayanfada Area, Ilesa, Osun State, CBS is supported by experienced teachers and a welcoming learning environment.
